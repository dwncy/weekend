**Weekend of November 7–8.** The rainy season is here, so this plan stays indoors and keeps the travel short. Do Kemang on Saturday: ICAD and the market are close to each other. Save Sunday for the Biennale or the old town, and leave early if the afternoon clouds build up. Hours and prices change fast, so check the official pages before you leave.

## 1. ICAD 16: Being: Becoming
**Date:** 8 October–8 November 2026; this is the final weekend
**Area:** South — grandKemang Jakarta
**Why go:** Indonesian Contemporary Art & Design closes on Sunday. More than 40 artists, designers and creative practitioners from 12 countries fill the hotel with exhibitions, talks and interactive work. It is free, so you can stay for one floor or all of them.
**Rough budget:** Free; register online first
**Weather:** Fully indoors; a good Saturday morning before the market gets busy.
**Source:** [ICAD 16 on NOW! Jakarta](https://www.nowjakarta.co.id/upcoming-events/one-more-week-to-explore-icad-16/)

## 2. MONOVERSE, A Cosmic Market
**Date:** 5–8 November 2026; daily 10am–10pm
**Area:** South — Lippo Mall Kemang
**Why go:** Market & Museum brings more than 140 Indonesian brands together: fashion, beauty, lifestyle, kids' things and food. Walk over after ICAD, set one shopping budget each, and eat your way through the stalls.
**Rough budget:** Free entry + whatever you buy
**Weather:** Inside the mall; the best plan when the afternoon rain arrives.
**Source:** [MONOVERSE on NOW! Jakarta](https://www.nowjakarta.co.id/upcoming-events/market-museum-brings-monoverse-to-lippo-mall-kemang-this-november/)

## 3. Jakarta Biennale 2026: Kebal
**Date:** 4 October–24 November 2026; on this weekend
**Area:** Central — Taman Ismail Marzuki, Cikini
**Why go:** This year's Biennale tells the stories behind Jakarta's resilience. The main show is in Gedung Trisno Soemardjo at TIM, and other programmes run in collective spaces across the city. Pick the main venue, then get coffee in Cikini.
**Rough budget:** Check the Biennale site for tickets and hours
**Weather:** Indoors at TIM; short walks outside between buildings.
**Source:** [Jakarta Biennale](https://jakartabiennale.id/)

## 4. Harmony in Diversity batik show
**Date:** 5–12 November 2026; on this weekend
**Area:** Old Town — House of Tugu, Jl. Kali Besar Barat No. 26
**Why go:** House of Tugu turns two on 7 November and celebrates with a Peranakan batik exhibition made with Bentara Budaya. Seven batik artists from four cities show their work. Combine it with a slow walk along Kali Besar if the sky stays dry.
**Rough budget:** Check with House of Tugu
**Weather:** The show is indoors; the Kali Besar walk is only for a dry hour.
**Source:** [Harmony in Diversity on NOW! Jakarta](https://www.nowjakarta.co.id/upcoming-events/harmony-in-diversity/)

## If you only do three things
1. **ICAD 16: Being: Becoming** — free, and this is your last chance to see it.
2. **MONOVERSE, A Cosmic Market** — next to ICAD, dry and open until 10pm.
3. **Jakarta Biennale 2026: Kebal** — the biggest art event in the city this month.
